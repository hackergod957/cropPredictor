      ABOUT SPROUT

      A small project,
      with a useful question.

      Sprout explores whether climate and soil data could help narrow down crop choices. It combines NASA POWER climate data, SoilGrids soil pH and a neural network trained on a crop recommendation dataset.

      The model considers temperature, humidity, rainfall and pH. It doesn't know your irrigation setup, nutrient levels or local crop varieties. The growing guides are written summaries with source links, not AI-generated advice.
